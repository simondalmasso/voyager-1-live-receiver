# Doppler

For radial velocity small relative to light speed, the classical approximation is `f_obs = f_ref * (1 - v_r/c)` under the documented positive-receding convention. A relativistic helper is included. The detector phase-rotates by offset plus linear drift and searches residual bins. Real coherent/free-running transponder behavior, Earth rotation, Earth orbit, observatory coordinates, and capture timing affect the observed carrier; operators must obtain a topocentric prediction.
